**Community Guidelines**

Last updated: **February 11, 2026**

Welcome to Xavvi. These Community Guidelines apply to all users and all content shared on our platform. They exist to keep Xavvi a safe, respectful, and enjoyable place for everyone.

By using Xavvi, you agree to follow these guidelines. **Xavvi has zero tolerance for objectionable content or abusive users.** Violations may result in content removal, account restrictions, or permanent bans.

1. **Content Standards**

   1. **Respect and Civility**
      * Treat all community members with respect.
      * Engage in constructive conversations even when you disagree.

   2. **No Hate Speech or Discrimination**
      * Content that promotes hatred, violence, or discrimination based on race, ethnicity, national origin, religion, gender, gender identity, sexual orientation, disability, age, or any other protected characteristic is prohibited.

   3. **No Harassment or Bullying**
      * Do not target, intimidate, or harass other users through comments, messages, or content.
      * Repeated unwanted contact, threats, or encouraging others to harass someone is prohibited.

   4. **No Violence or Graphic Content**
      * Do not post content that depicts, promotes, or glorifies violence, self-harm, or dangerous activities.
      * Graphic, gory, or shocking content intended to disturb viewers is not allowed.

   5. **No Nudity or Sexual Content**
      * Sexually explicit or pornographic content is strictly prohibited.
      * Content that sexualizes minors in any way will result in immediate and permanent removal and may be reported to law enforcement.

   6. **No Dangerous or Illegal Activities**
      * Do not post content that promotes illegal activities, including drug use, underage drinking, or any activity that violates applicable laws.
      * Content promoting dangerous challenges or stunts that could result in physical harm is prohibited.

   7. **Intellectual Property**
      * Only share content that you own or have the right to share.
      * Do not upload copyrighted music, videos, images, or other material without proper authorization.

2. **Prohibited Behaviors**

   1. **Spam and Manipulation**
      * Do not post repetitive, misleading, or irrelevant content to artificially increase engagement.
      * Automated activity, including bots, fake accounts, and coordinated inauthentic behavior, is prohibited.

   2. **Impersonation**
      * Do not impersonate other users, public figures, brands, or organizations.
      * Parody and fan accounts must be clearly identified as such.

   3. **Scams and Fraud**
      * Do not use Xavvi to promote scams, phishing attempts, or fraudulent schemes.
      * Misleading claims about products, services, or financial opportunities are prohibited.

   4. **Misinformation**
      * Do not deliberately spread false or misleading information that could cause harm, particularly regarding health, safety, elections, or civic processes.

   5. **Privacy Violations**
      * Do not share personal information of others without their consent, including real names, addresses, phone numbers, or financial information.
      * Do not record or share private conversations or interactions without consent.

   6. **Platform Manipulation**
      * Do not attempt to manipulate platform features, algorithms, or reward systems through deceptive means.
      * Exploiting bugs or vulnerabilities rather than reporting them is prohibited.

3. **Content Moderation Process**

   1. **Review Methods**
      * We use a combination of automated systems and human moderators to review content on Xavvi.
      * Content may be reviewed proactively or in response to user reports.

   2. **Content Removal**
      * Content that violates these guidelines will be removed promptly.
      * In some cases, content may be restricted (e.g., age-gated or limited in distribution) rather than removed entirely.

   3. **Appeals Process**
      * If your content is removed or your account is restricted, you will be notified with the reason.
      * You may appeal the decision by contacting us at support@xavvi.com within 30 days of the action.
      * Appeals will be reviewed by a different moderator than the one who made the original decision.
      * You will receive a response to your appeal within a reasonable timeframe.

4. **Consequences for Violations**

   1. **Warnings**
      * First-time or minor violations may result in a warning and removal of the offending content.

   2. **Temporary Restrictions**
      * Repeated or more serious violations may result in temporary restrictions on your account, including limits on posting, commenting, or messaging.

   3. **Temporary Bans**
      * Continued violations after warnings may result in a temporary ban from the platform.

   4. **Permanent Bans**
      * Severe violations, including any content that sexualizes minors, credible threats of violence, or repeated violations after prior enforcement, will result in permanent account termination.
      * Permanently banned users may not create new accounts.

   5. **Legal Reporting**
      * Content that involves the exploitation of minors, credible threats, or other illegal activity will be reported to the appropriate law enforcement authorities.

5. **Reporting**

   1. **How to Report**
      * Use the in-app reporting feature available on all content and profiles to flag potential violations.
      * You may also report violations by contacting us at support@xavvi.com.

   2. **What Happens When You Report**
      * All reports are reviewed by our moderation team.
      * Reports are confidential. We do not reveal the identity of the reporter to the reported user.
      * We will act on objectionable content reports within 24 hours by removing the content and ejecting the user who provided the offending content.

   3. **False Reports**
      * Submitting false or abusive reports may result in action against your account.

6. **Minors and Safety**

   1. Users under 18 years of age are not permitted to use Xavvi. In Australia, users must be at least 16 years of age.
   2. We take additional steps to protect young users, including restricting certain content and features for users under 18.
   3. Any attempt to contact minors in an inappropriate or exploitative manner will result in an immediate permanent ban and a report to law enforcement.

7. **Child Safety Standards (CSAE)**

   Xavvi maintains strict standards against Child Sexual Abuse and Exploitation (CSAE) in accordance with applicable laws and platform policies.

   1. **Zero Tolerance**: Any content depicting, promoting, or facilitating the sexual exploitation or abuse of minors is strictly prohibited and will result in immediate permanent account termination.
   2. **Detection and Reporting**: We actively work to detect and remove CSAM (Child Sexual Abuse Material). All confirmed or suspected CSAM is reported to the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC) via CyberTipline and to relevant law enforcement authorities.
   3. **User Reporting**: Users can report suspected child exploitation content or behavior through our in-app reporting feature or by contacting our dedicated child safety team at **csae@xavvi.com**.
   4. **Cooperation with Authorities**: We cooperate fully with law enforcement investigations related to child exploitation.
   5. **Child Safety Point of Contact**: For child safety concerns, contact our designated Child Safety Officer at **csae@xavvi.com**.

8. **Contact Us**

   1. If you have questions about these Community Guidelines or need to report a concern, please contact us at support@xavvi.com.
   2. For legal inquiries, contact legal@xavvi.com.
