1. **Information We Collect**

We collect information in three ways: (i) information you provide to us, (ii) information collected automatically when you use the Services, and (iii) information we receive from other sources.

   1. **Information You Provide**

   When you create an account, provide additional information in connection with rewards programs (such as Reward Boost, as defined in our Terms of Service), or otherwise use the Services, you may provide information such as:

   1. **Account Information,** for example, your name, date of birth, username, password, email address, phone number, and profile image.  
      2. **Account Recovery Information,** where applicable (such as for guest accounts), a recovery passphrase and/or a hashed version used to help you regain access to your account. This is for account recovery only.  
      3. **User Content,** content you create or submit through the Services, including comments, messages, video messages, reviews, and participation in Brand surveys, research initiatives, promotions, marketing campaigns, events, or contests.  
      4. **AI Interactions,** where available, prompts, responses to questionnaires, and interactions with AI-enabled features.  
      5. **Purchase and Rewards Information,** such as purchase confirmations, delivery status, and rewards/credit activity when you engage with shopping or reward experiences through the Services. Payment card details are generally processed by third parties (such as Brands or payment processors) rather than stored by Xavvi.  
      6. **Following Information,** information about the accounts, creators, or channels you follow or follow you.  
      7. **Your Settings,** account settings and communication preferences.  
      8. **Credits and Activity Information:** your in-app credits balance and activity history within the Services, such as credits earned through engagement with content, campaigns, or shopping features.  
      9. **Optional Demographic and Preference Information:** where you choose to provide it, information such as age, gender, race/ethnicity, location (e.g., city/country), and interests, which may be used to personalize experiences and rewards participation.
   2. **Automatically Collected Information**

   When you use the Services, we automatically collect certain information, including:

      1. **Usage Information,** information about your interactions with the Services, such as how you engage with content and advertisements, the duration and frequency of your use, and, where applicable, shopping-related activity provided to us by Brands in connection with the Reward Boost program. This includes behavioral analytics events such as video watch time, content interactions (likes, shares, comments), navigation patterns, and tap or click events, which are used to improve the Services and personalize your experience.
      2. **Technical Information,** information from your device, network, and application, including IP address, user agent, mobile carrier, time zone settings, device model, operating system, network type, screen resolution, app and file names and types, and device identifiers. We may generate and assign locally-created device identifiers or user identifiers and use this information to understand usage patterns across devices, where permitted by law. We do not collect or use Apple's Identifier for Advertisers (IDFA) or any platform advertising identifier.
      3. **Location Information,** approximate location information derived from your device or network, such as SIM card region, IP address, or device system settings.  
      4. **Metadata,** metadata automatically generated in connection with your user content, messages, or AI interactions, such as when, where, and how content was created or a message or prompt was sent. Metadata may also include information, such as your username, that allows your content to be associated with your account.  
      5. **Push Notification Identifiers,** if you enable push notifications, we collect a device-specific push notification identifier issued by Apple Push Notification service (APNs) or Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM). This identifier is used solely to deliver push notifications to your device and is deleted from our servers when you sign out or revoke notification permissions.
      6. **Cookies and Similar Technologies,** we and our service providers and business partners use cookies and similar technologies (such as web beacons, pixels, SDKs, and similar tools) to provide and enhance the Services, analyze usage, personalize content and advertising, measure performance, and promote the Services on other websites and applications.
   3. **Information From Other Sources**

   We may receive information described in this Privacy Policy from third parties, including:

      1. **Third-Party Login Providers,** if you choose to register or log in using a third-party service, we may receive information from that service, such as your public profile information and email address.  
      2. **Advertising and Measurement Partners,** advertisers, publishers, analytics providers, and measurement partners may provide information about your interactions with their content, websites, or applications, including pages visited, products or services purchased, or apps downloaded. This information may include advertising identifiers, hashed contact information, or cookie-based identifiers, and is used to measure advertising effectiveness and deliver relevant ads in accordance with your settings.  
      3. **Commerce and Fulfillment Partners,** sellers, payment processors, and transaction fulfillment providers may provide information such as payment confirmations and delivery status for products purchased through shopping features on the Services.
